A letter from my heart

Hey, you. 🤍

Happy birthday to one of the most precious people I've found. It's honestly wild to think that a year ago, we were strangers, and now you're someone who has such a special place in my heart.

I never expected an online friendship to mean this much to me. Somewhere between our random conversations, silly jokes, late-night talks, and the secrets we've trusted each other with, you became one of my safest people. There are things we've shared with each other that we haven't told anyone else, and I'll always treasure the trust that comes with that.

You're not “just an online friend” to me. You're a real part of my life, someone I worry about, root for, laugh with, and want to see happy. I'm so grateful that out of all the people in this huge world, our paths crossed.

Your birthday feels like a festival to me because how could I not celebrate the day someone as wonderful as you came into this world? I hope this next chapter gives you gentleness on hard days, reasons to laugh until your stomach hurts, people who love you well, and every little thing your heart has been wishing for.

Thank you for trusting me with your stories, your soft spots, your secrets, and simply you. No matter how life changes, I hope you never forget that I'm cheering for you. Here's to us, to this beautiful little friendship, and to all the memories we haven't made yet.

Happy birthday, my precious girl. I'm so glad you exist. ♡
